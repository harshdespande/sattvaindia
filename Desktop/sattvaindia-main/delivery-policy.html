Delivery Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Delivery Policy explains how orders placed with Sattva India (OVP Quality Foods) are delivered to hotels, restaurants, caterers, canteens, hostels, retailers, and distributors.

1. Delivery Areas

We operate daily supply routes across Pune and PCMC, including but not limited to:

  • Nigdi & Pradhikaran
  • Bhosari & Chakan MIDC
  • Hinjewadi IT Corridor
  • Wakad, Baner & Kothrud
  • Viman Nagar & Hadapsar

Deliveries outside these routes, including other cities/states or export shipments, can be arranged on request — please mention your location in the enquiry form or while confirming your order.

2. Delivery Timelines

Standard institutional orders within our regular Pune/PCMC routes are typically delivered within 1–3 business days of order confirmation, depending on order size and the requested delivery date. Custom formulations or large bulk/export orders may require a longer lead time, which will be communicated at the time of quotation.

3. Delivery Date Selection

The order form allows you to select a preferred delivery date. This date is treated as a request and is confirmed (or adjusted if needed) by our team during order confirmation.

4. Delivery Charges

Delivery within our standard Pune/PCMC routes is generally included for qualifying bulk orders; charges for smaller orders or deliveries outside standard routes will be communicated transparently before order confirmation.

5. Receiving Your Order

Please ensure an authorized person is available to receive and inspect the delivery at the confirmed location and time. Any visible damage or shortage should be noted at the time of delivery and reported to us within 24–48 hours (see our Refund Policy).

6. Delays

While we make every effort to meet confirmed delivery dates, delays may occasionally occur due to factors like weather, transport availability, or raw material sourcing. We will proactively inform you via WhatsApp or phone if a delay is expected.

7. Order Tracking

As we are a growing institutional supplier, live order tracking is not yet available on this website. For an update on your order status, please reach out to us directly via WhatsApp or phone — we're happy to help.
