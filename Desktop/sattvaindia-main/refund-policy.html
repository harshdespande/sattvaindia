Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

As Sattva India operates on a quote-and-confirm basis rather than an online checkout, refund and cancellation terms are agreed on a per-order basis at the time of confirmation. This page outlines our general approach.

1. Order Confirmation

No order is considered final until it has been confirmed in writing (via WhatsApp or email) by both parties, including agreed pricing, quantities, and delivery date. Cancellation terms below apply from the point of confirmation, not from the initial enquiry.

2. Cancellations Before Dispatch

Orders may be cancelled or amended free of charge if the request is made before the order has been packed for dispatch. Please contact us as early as possible via phone or WhatsApp if you need to cancel or change an order.

3. Cancellations After Dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched for delivery, it generally cannot be cancelled. In such cases, please refer to Section 4 (Quality Issues) below.

4. Quality Issues & Returns

Given the nature of food and spice products, returns are only accepted in the following cases:

  • Products delivered are damaged, spoiled, or do not match the confirmed order
  • A quality or packaging defect is identified and reported within 24–48 hours of delivery

Please report such issues immediately via WhatsApp or phone with photos of the affected product, and our team will arrange a replacement, credit note, or refund as appropriate, depending on what was agreed for that order.

5. Refund Method

Where a refund is agreed, it will be processed using the same payment method originally used, or as otherwise mutually agreed, within a reasonable timeframe after the issue is resolved.

6. Bulk / Custom Orders

Custom formulations, large bulk orders, or advance-paid orders may carry specific cancellation terms communicated at the time of order confirmation, which will take precedence over the general terms above.

7. Contact for Refund Requests

All refund, replacement, or cancellation requests should be raised directly with our team via phone or WhatsApp for the fastest resolution.
