Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Sattva India ("we", "us", "our") respects the privacy of everyone who visits this website or submits an enquiry to us. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit the bulk procurement enquiry form on this website, we collect:

  • Business name and contact person name
  • Phone number (WhatsApp) and, optionally, email address
  • Business sector/type and delivery location
  • Requested products, quantities, delivery date, and any special instructions

We do not collect payment information, passwords, or any sensitive personal data through this website, as we do not operate an online store, login, or checkout system.

2. How We Use This Information

Information submitted is used solely to:

  • Prepare and share a quotation for your enquiry
  • Contact you via WhatsApp, email, or phone to confirm order details
  • Maintain an internal record of enquiries for follow-up and business planning

Enquiry details are recorded in an internal, access-controlled Google Sheet used only by the Sattva India team. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. WhatsApp & Email

When you choose to submit your enquiry via WhatsApp or Email, you are redirected to those platforms directly from your device. Any messages exchanged are subject to WhatsApp's or your email provider's own privacy practices in addition to this policy.

4. Data Retention

Enquiry records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary for business, accounting, or legal purposes. You may request that your enquiry record be deleted by contacting us directly (see contact details below).

5. Cookies & Analytics

This website may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand overall visitor traffic (e.g. number of visits, popular pages). No personally identifying data is collected through analytics beyond what you voluntarily submit via the enquiry form.

6. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to review, correct, or request deletion of the information you've submitted to us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
