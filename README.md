# Sattva India - OVP Quality Foods

🌶️ **Professional B2B Wholesale Food & Spice Supply Platform**

## About

Sattva India (OVP Quality Foods) is a FSSAI & ISO 9001:2015 certified wholesale spice supplier and institutional food partner based in Pradhikaran, Nigdi, Pune, Maharashtra.

We specialize in:
- Premium ground masala blends (20+ varieties)
- Whole spices and dry fruits (24+ items)
- Commercial sauces and condiments
- Papad, pickles, and pantry staples
- Custom formulations for hotels, restaurants, and exporters

## Features

✅ **Interactive Product Catalog** - 65+ institutional-grade products  
✅ **Multi-language Support** - English, Marathi, Hindi  
✅ **Quote Drawer System** - Build custom bulk orders  
✅ **WhatsApp Integration** - Direct order submission  
✅ **Compliance Portal** - View FSSAI, ISO, and COA certificates  
✅ **Delivery Grid** - Daily Pune & PCMC routes  
✅ **Mobile Optimized** - Responsive design for all devices
