Company Heritage

Bridging Pure Farmlands with Commercial Kitchens

Founded with a commitment to pure, unadulterated spices and institutional food supplies, Sattva India (OVP Quality Foods) has grown to become the preferred partner for leading hospitality groups, caterers, educational messes, and international spice distributors across Maharashtra and beyond.

Unlike standard commercial suppliers, our procurement model originates directly from India's primary spice hubs—Byadgi and Guntur for chili, Salem and Sangli for turmeric, Unjha for cumin, and Kerala for Tellicherry black pepper. Our low-temperature milling protects essential oils, volatility, and natural pungency without synthetic fillers, artificial starch, or artificial coloring agents.
